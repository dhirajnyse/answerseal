AnswerSeal should not rush into private beta by adding more noise. The gate shows the smallest launch checklist that protects trust: proof, data, access, support, and rollback.

01 Confirm go areas

Core verification, reports, registry, feedback, and governance pages are ready for buyer conversations.

02 Name every hold

Each remaining launch risk gets an owner, a reason, a next action, and a readiness percentage.

03 Define launch threshold

Private beta starts when backend, accounts, support, and pilot proof reach a defensible threshold.

04 Record the receipt

The final beta decision becomes a reusable launch receipt for future countries, teams, and environments.
