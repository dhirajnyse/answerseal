AnswerSeal should learn from what actually happened, but never by silently rewriting trusted behavior. Each signal is translated into a proposal with proof, scope, owner, and rollback path.

01 Outcome

Capture whether the answer helped the buyer, reviewer, sales team, or governance process move forward.

02 Proposal

Suggest a prompt, connector, evidence, or wording improvement without changing production automatically.

03 Boundary

Classify the signal as org-private, evidence-only, aggregate-safe, or blocked until privacy review.

04 Approval

Promote the change only with owner review, bench result, rationale, and rollback note attached.
