Signal rules

Useful beta learning is measured, owned, and reversible.

The signal ledger protects AnswerSeal from learning too quickly from too little data. Every signal gets a boundary before it becomes a product, support, pricing, or automation decision.

01 Attach source

Each signal points to a sealed report, buyer comment, support note, or weekly receipt.

02 Name owner

Every signal has one person who decides whether to keep, fix, repeat, hold, or expand.

03 Set boundary

Customer context stays tenant-local unless it is approved as aggregate-safe learning.

04 Record decision

Signals end as continue, pause, narrow, expand, convert, or hold decisions.
