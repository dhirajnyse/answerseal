Conversion model

The paid ask should come from proof, not pressure.

A founder should not guess when to ask for a paid pilot. The conversion room turns pilot evidence into a buyer-safe packet, plain terms, a named owner, and a receipt that says why the ask is ready or held.

Signal Close readiness is visible

Proof health, buyer activity, open owner tasks, and weekly decisions combine into one conversion signal.

Packet Buyer proof is packaged

Verified answers, safe excerpts, source coverage, and pilot outcomes become a packet the buyer can trust.

Terms The ask is bounded

Scope, timeframe, success measure, price anchor, data boundary, and next implementation step stay explicit.

Receipt The close has memory

Ask, response, next owner action, proof gap, decision, and follow-up path become one reusable receipt.

Founder rhythm

One weekly question: can this become paid?

The product stays simple by making the commercial step visible. If proof is strong, package the ask. If proof is weak, hold the ask and show the next fix.
