Approval anatomy

Approval is a packet, not a button.

A tune can ship only when the approver sees why it exists, what evidence passed, where policy still applies, and how to reverse it. That keeps closed-loop learning strong without making it reckless.

Change What will move

Show the threshold, rule, route, or warning level that will change if the owner approves.

Confidence Why it is safe

Pair reviewer confidence with evidence coverage, policy alignment, and test status.

Rationale Owner explains

Capture the human reason for approval so future reviewers understand the decision.

Approval receipt Ship or reverse

Record approver, scope, evidence, test result, changed threshold, and rollback path.

Human control

World-class AI governance should feel boring in the best way.

Approval Lab keeps the product simple: every improvement has an owner, every owner sees the risk, and every shipped tune keeps a receipt that protects local policy, regional rollout, and future audits.

Approve Ship narrowly

Ask Need rationale

Revise Reduce scope

The tune is promising, but it should become smaller or better tested first.

Reject Protect trust

If proof, policy, or rollback are weak, the tune becomes a lesson instead of a release.
