Weekly rhythm

Every beta week ends with a receipt, not a feeling.

The cohort room keeps learning disciplined. Customer signals are useful only when they are tied to a workspace, an owner, a risk state, and a decision that can be explained later.

01 Check setup

Confirm workspace access, evidence files, first sealed report, buyer link status, and customer owner.

02 Measure value

Capture saved time, trust-score lift, reusable answer count, buyer unblock, and support load.

03 Hold risky changes

Keep pricing, automation, shared learning, and integrations behind owner approval until cohort proof stays healthy.

04 Decide simply

Continue, pause, narrow, or expand. One decision per cohort, with the reason visible.

Cohort rule

Do not scale the beta until the first cohort becomes boringly healthy.

v2.0 protects the product from premature expansion. AnswerSeal learns from real users, but every useful signal is routed through local context, owner approval, and a weekly decision.
