Tuning anatomy

A tuning task is not a suggestion. It is a governed change.

The queue keeps closed-loop AI calm: outcome drift becomes a small edit, a named owner reviews it, a test proves the change is safe, and the receipt keeps rollback one decision away.

Drift Why tune

False blocks, reviewer load, stale warnings, or weak lift must explain why the guard needs a change.

Edit Small threshold move

Owners change one clear rule: source age, warning sensitivity, replay scope, or approval route.

Test Bench before rollout

The proposed tune must pass scenarios before it can influence buyer-facing answers.

Tuning receipt Old to new

The receipt records old value, new value, expected lift, approver, and reversal path.

Calm control

Learning loops should improve quietly, with humans still in charge.

Shared learning can help every organization only when each tenant can hold, tune, or reverse the change locally. The tuning queue turns reinforcement into controlled product operations.

Approve Ready tune

Low risk, clean test plan, named owner, and clear reversal path.

Revise Too broad

Split the tune into a smaller threshold change before it reaches the workflow.

Hold Not enough signal

Keep the existing guard until more accepted outcomes or reviewer evidence arrives.

Reject Trust would drop

If the tune weakens proof or policy, it becomes a lesson instead of a release.
