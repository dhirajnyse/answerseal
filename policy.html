Gateway method

Every AI path needs a rule before it gets reach.

The gateway turns scattered governance into a simple decision receipt: what path is being used, what rule controls it, where it can travel, and who owns the final approval.

01 Path

Name the answer, prompt, connector, agent route, learning signal, or rollout environment.

02 Rule

Attach the controlling policy, approval gate, source requirement, or privacy boundary.

03 Scope

Define where the output can travel: local workspace, buyer portal, region, network, or blocked zone.

04 Receipt

Keep the decision, owner, evidence, and next action visible before reuse or promotion.

Decision language

The safest policy is visible before the action.

AnswerSeal should feel simple because the hard governance work is compressed into three visible decisions that every team can understand without reading a policy manual.

Allow Ready to use

The answer or workflow has proof, owner approval, source support, and a receipt.

Hold Needs owner decision

The path may be useful, but privacy, source, region, or reviewer status must be resolved.

Block Do not release

The action cannot travel because it is unsupported, over-automated, or outside policy.
