Friction rules

A beta blocker is not useful until it has an owner and a next action.

The queue keeps learning calm: one class, one owner, one smallest fix, one confidence level, and one expansion decision.

01 Classify blocker

Sort friction into setup, proof, buyer, support, pricing, or expansion categories.

02 Assign owner

Every blocker gets one accountable owner instead of a vague team-wide concern.

03 Choose smallest fix

Route the minimum useful change before adding new product complexity.

04 Gate expansion

Invites wait until the queue proves the fix reduced support drag or buyer risk.
