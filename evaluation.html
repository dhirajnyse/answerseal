QA method

The bench asks the hard questions before buyers do.

Each artifact is tested for proof, risk, clarity, compliance sensitivity, and whether a new version is actually safer than the sealed baseline.

01 Test cases

Reusable checks follow every answer, prompt, workflow, or agent output.

02 Comparison

Candidate outputs are judged against source coverage and the approved baseline.

03 Challenge

Unsupported claims, weak proof, and overconfident wording are pushed into review.

04 Gate

Promotion is blocked when a new version is weaker than the sealed answer.

Regression gate

Better is required.

The bench does not reward novelty. It rewards safer, clearer, better sourced answers that can survive legal, security, buyer, and governance review.

Baseline Sealed report

Candidate New output

Decision Promote or hold
